PCTALKER PC Speaker - Hungarian - DEMO

This addon demonstrates the audio quality and PC-speaker sound characteristics of the Hungarian PCTALKER PC Speaker version developed by J. Kiraly between 1989 and 1991.

  • Fast envelope (no PWM carrier)
  • True PWM (PC-speaker acoustic emulation)
  • Real PWM 48 kHz
  • Real PWM 48 kHz + 3.5 kHz filter
